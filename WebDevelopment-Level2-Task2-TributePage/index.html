His Story

A Life of Purpose

Dr. Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam was born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu. Through education, determination and a deep interest in science, he built a career that would leave a lasting mark on India's scientific progress.

After studying aeronautical engineering at the Madras Institute of Technology, Kalam worked with India's space and defence organisations. At ISRO, he played a significant role in the development of the SLV-III launch vehicle, which successfully placed the Rohini satellite into orbit in 1980.

He later contributed to India's indigenous guided-missile programmes at the Defence Research and Development Organisation. His scientific achievements and commitment to national development earned him recognition as the "Missile Man of India."

In 2002, Kalam became the 11th President of India. During and after his presidency, he continued to encourage students to pursue knowledge, think creatively and contribute to society. His life remains an example of how learning and service can work together to create a meaningful legacy.

Milestones

His Remarkable Journey

Important moments in a life devoted to science, leadership and education.

  1. 1931

    A Beginning in Rameswaram

    Abdul Kalam was born on 15 October in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu.

  2. 1980

    SLV-III Success

    The SLV-III programme successfully placed the Rohini satellite into orbit, marking an important achievement for India's space programme.

  3. 1997

    Bharat Ratna

    He received the Bharat Ratna, India's highest civilian award, in recognition of his contributions.

  4. 2002

    President of India

    He became the 11th President of India on 25 July 2002.

  5. 2007

    A Lasting Public Legacy

    His presidential term concluded on 25 July 2007, but his educational outreach continued.

  6. 2015

    Remembered Across Generations

    Kalam passed away on 27 July 2015 while addressing students at the Indian Institute of Management Shillong. His contributions continue to inspire students and educators.

Beyond the Presidency

A Legacy That Continues

Dr. Kalam showed that scientific curiosity, dedication to education and a commitment to public service can make a lasting difference. His journey encourages future generations to learn continuously, set meaningful goals and use their knowledge for the benefit of others.
